Overview

As a Scholarship Mentor at BINUS University, Currently, I guide three mentees across various academic disciplines, offering personalized support to help them master specialized courses such as Speech Recognition, Compilation Techniques, and Algorithm and Programming.

02

About Mentoring

  • What Courses that I teachingData Structures, Database Technology, Deep Learning, Compilation Techniques, Network Security Fundamental, Speech Recognition, Operating System, Algorithm and Programming, Algorithm Design and Analysis, and Computational Physics
  • What i doAs a mentor, I ensure that I thoroughly review the course materials before each session so I can effectively guide my mentees through complex topics. During our meetings, I focus on clarifying concepts they find difficult to grasp. However, my role goes beyond traditional tutoring; I act as a peer learning partner. I actively listen to their academic struggles, provide motivation, and share practical tips and customized study notes to help them navigate their coursework and overcome challenges.
  • ChallengesDuring my mentorship, I encountered several challenges that tested my adaptability and communication skills. Because I tutored courses I had taken a while ago, I frequently had to rapidly recall and relearn past material to provide accurate guidance. Additionally, I faced interpersonal hurdles, such as mentees lacking motivation or being completely unresponsive when trying to schedule our initial sessions. Navigating these situations taught me how to be proactive and empathetic in managing student engagement.
  • SolutionsTo overcome these challenges, I took a proactive approach. I began reviewing and relearning the older course materials well in advance, giving me ample time to fully recall and grasp the concepts before teaching them. To tackle the lack of motivation, I made the sessions more engaging by connecting the academic material to the mentees' personal interests and real-world scenarios, making the learning process much more enjoyable. Finally, when dealing with unresponsive mentees, I took the initiative to reach out to them in person. This offline approach allowed me to understand their situations better, establish direct communication, and successfully initiate our mentoring journey.
03

What i learns

This experience taught me a great deal about effective teaching and the discipline required to continuously relearn and reinforce my own knowledge. I developed strong interpersonal skills by learning how to approach new people, build rapport, and motivate mentees to actively participate in the sessions. Additionally, this role significantly honed my problem-solving abilities, teaching me how to navigate and resolve unexpected situations quickly and accurately.
