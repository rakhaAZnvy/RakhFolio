LokalLoop

A circular logistics coordination layer for waste banks and informal collectors: reports via WhatsApp, low-cost IoT drop points, and AI-based route optimization.

01

The problem

Indonesian's plastic recycling chain is lopsided. There is plenty of waste, yet recyclers still lack raw material and the country still imports plastic waste. Upstream, informal collectors look for supply without structured information, wasting time and energy on the road. Downstream, the daily volume of waste banks often falls short of the minimum order quantity (MOQ) required by large buyers, so material piles up as inventory that has not yet become income.

The team's initial survey of 41 respondents shows the opportunity: residents are willing to participate if the process is not a hassle.

76%still mix plastic bottles with other waste
59%are willing to make extra effort given a clear incentive
66%prefer WhatsApp-based pickup over a new app
02

Solution: LokalLoop

LokalLoop is not a new fleet. It is a coordination layer that gives information and routes to the informal collectors who already operate, following a zero-friction principle: residents do not need to download an app.

  • WhatsApp bot with NLP Residents send a location pin and a free-text message. An LLM turns the message into structured data (estimated weight and pickup time), while coordinates are taken directly from WhatsApp's location feature to stay precise.
  • LokalBox, a Frugal IoT drop point An ESP32-based box with a keypad and one-time PIN, a load cell for weighing, and an ultrasonic sensor for fill level. When it reaches an 80% threshold, LokalBox registers itself as a candidate pickup node.
  • CVRP route optimization Google OR-Tools and a real road-distance matrix from OSRM build collector routes with a cart load limit of 150 kg per cycle.
  • Micro-Pooling (beta, Phase 2) Inventories from several waste banks are pooled virtually so the industrial MOQ is reached sooner.
03

How it works

The system has three layers: edge (ESP32 and sensors), backend (Node.js with TypeScript, PostgreSQL, and a WhatsApp API webhook), and an AI layer (Python FastAPI). The LLM acts only as a data extraction layer; route decisions are computed entirely by the optimization algorithm.

  1. Residents report via WhatsApp or drop material into a LokalBox.
  2. Messages and sensor data become structured requests. Ambiguous messages are confirmed back with the resident.
  3. CVRP groups all requests into efficient pickup routes.
  4. Collectors run the route and the material enters the waste bank as digital inventory.
  5. Micro-Pooling combines inventories across waste banks toward industrial scale.
04

Test results

Phase 1 was tested quantitatively on 145 pickup requests over five days in the Serpong Utara and Pinang area, compared against a scenario where pickups follow the order reports arrive (FCFS).

58.74%reduction in collector travel distance, from 176.46 to 72.48 km per day
92%NLP extraction accuracy (92 of 100 fields across 50 test messages)
20/20ESP32-to-backend transmissions succeeded; load cell error 0.1 to 0.3 g
34 dayssimulated projection to reach the 2,000 kg MOQ with Micro-Pooling, versus 57 to 80 days without aggregation

Cart capacity utilization was the same in both scenarios (73.48%), so the hypothesis that utilization would improve was not supported. CVRP's advantage is distance: the same volume is served with much shorter trips. The Micro-Pooling figures are still mathematical projections, not field tests.

05

Limitations and next steps

The LokalBox tested is still a version 1.0 prototype (cardboard and styrofoam) in a controlled environment, and NLP extraction depends on a third-party API. The study did not yet measure fuel consumption, collector fatigue, material quality, settlement mechanisms, or governance among waste banks.

Next steps are testing LokalBox in public spaces with varied materials and networks, then developing an operating model for Micro-Pooling together with stakeholders. The roadmap has three phases: habit building and material acquisition, B2B supply chain orchestration, then financial integration and ESG reporting.
