CheckShop

CheckShop is a Streamlit app for analyzing English online shop reviews. It compares Logistic Regression, Naive Bayes, XGBoost, and fine-tuned BERT; BERT achieved the highest macro F1-score in the evaluation, at 94.4%.

01

Overview

CheckShop helps sellers understand customer feedback without reading every review manually. The models were trained on the BanglaEcomReviewCorpus dataset, using the English translations of its reviews. After cleaning, it contains about 8,587 reviews labeled positive, negative, or neutral. A user types a review, and the app returns the predicted sentiment from all four models at once.

02

What it does

  • Multi-model prediction Enter an English shop review to get a sentiment prediction.
  • Probability charts Each model shows a probability bar chart for the three classes, plus a combined probability table.
  • Compare four models Review predictions from Logistic Regression, Naive Bayes, XGBoost, and BERT.
  • View model performance BERT achieved a macro F1-score of 94.4% in the reported evaluation.
