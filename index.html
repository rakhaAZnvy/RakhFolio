VisioAttend

VisioAttend is a desktop attendance system created for a computer vision course. It registers students, recognizes faces through a webcam, and records daily attendance. It is built with Python, Tkinter, and OpenCV, using Haar Cascade for face detection and LBPH for face recognition.

CheckShop

CheckShop was my first project to make a web with many NLP models and analyzing the NLP models to know which one model is the best for CheckShop, CheckShop is an Streamlit website application that analyzes the sentiment of English online shop reviews using four models side by side: Logistic Regression, Naive Bayes, XGBoost, and a fine-tuned BERT. After we analyze the models, the best result came from BERT with a macro F1-score of 94.4%.

ICISS 2026 - IEEE Publication

I served as a co-author and presenter for our research paper titled "Comparative Evaluation of Precision and Relevance in Traditional, Graph, and LLM-Prompted Recommender Models." In this study, my team and I evaluated five different recommender models to determine whether assessing them requires a combination of accuracy and relevance metrics, or if one is sufficient to enhance the user experience. We concluded that evaluating recommender models necessitates both metric types to strike a balance between mathematical accuracy and real-world user satisfaction. This paper was presented at the ICISS 2026 conference and officially published in IEEE. To read the full paper, please click the button below.
